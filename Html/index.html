Explore the World's Highest Peaks

Celebrating 40 years of leading successful expeditions in 2026, Alpine Ascents International, Inc. is a leader in the climbing industry and has been instrumental in setting guiding standards in the international climbing community. We offer more than 30 expeditions and 50 training courses in 14 countries, including the Arctic and Antarctic regions. From three-day climbs to ascents of the highest mountains around the world, our mission is to help climbers achieve their personal aspirations through quality, challenging, safety-conscious and fun expeditions.
