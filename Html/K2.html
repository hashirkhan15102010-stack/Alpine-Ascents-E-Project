KARAKORAM MOUNTAIN

Where the sky begins and only the bold arrive.

Rising 8,611 metres (28,251 ft) above sea level, K2 is the second-highest mountain on Earth and one of the most demanding peaks in the world. Its steep pyramid shape, unpredictable weather and technical climbing have earned it the nickname “The Savage Mountain.”

History

From first survey to summit

K2 was first recorded during the 1856 Great Trigonometrical Survey, which gave it the label “K2” because it was the second peak measured in the Karakoram. For nearly a century it resisted every attempt, until the 1954 Italian expedition finally reached the summit.

Since then it has remained far less climbed than Everest and is widely regarded as the harder and more dangerous of the two mountains.

First ascent: 31 July 1954 by Achille Compagnoni and Lino Lacedelli
First winter ascent: 16 January 2021
Best climbing season: June to August

The Journey

From Base Camp to the Bottleneck

Most expeditions fly to Islamabad, then to Skardu, before trekking for several days along the Baltoro Glacier. Passing Concordia, one of the most spectacular mountain viewpoints on the planet, climbers eventually reach K2 Base Camp.

The standard climbing route is the Abruzzi Spur, which includes the dangerous Bottleneck section near the summit.

Safety

Your Safety Is Our Summit

  • Acclimatize slowly and climb high, sleep low.
  • Know the warning signs of altitude sickness and descend immediately if needed.
  • Check the weather daily and adjust plans to changing conditions.
  • Stay hydrated and carry the essentials for high-altitude travel.
